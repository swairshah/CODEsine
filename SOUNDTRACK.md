# Soundtrack proposals

For each of the thirteen stages, this lists the sound bites (something said at a launch, or an advert) and three or four songs for the background music. Every link opens on YouTube; sound-bite links start at the moment listed.

Timestamps come from each video's YouTube transcript, read in October 2026. Captions are mostly auto-generated, so a line can start a second or two before or after the time shown. Where a video has no transcript, the entry says so.

★ marks a song Apple itself used for that product or its launch, in an advert or at the event. Songs without ★ are big hits from the same year. "Fan upload" means someone other than Apple or the artist posted the video, so the link may stop working.

All of this is copyrighted. The app now selects one song and a short sound bite per stage, stored in `audio/custom/` and configured in `js/soundtrack.js`. `audio/custom/sources.json` records the exact selections and refined clip times. The original placeholders remain available as fallbacks. Obtain the required permissions before publishing or redistributing the recordings. YouTube playback is optional and uses a visible embedded player.

## 1. Apple I, 1976

**Sound bites**

- [Steve Wozniak remembers building the first Apple computer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dz4aiU4FiIM), 0:00–3:50. No transcript, so pick the moment by ear. Fan upload (FORA.tv).
- [An original Apple I running, "Hello World"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vGFyy-jpI9s), 0:00–5:53. The machine itself at work, no speech. Nexon Computer Museum.

**Songs**

1. [Jean-Michel Jarre, "Oxygène, Pt. 4"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kSIMVnPA994). 1976, an all-synthesiser hit and the most "computer" sound of the year.
2. [ABBA, "Dancing Queen"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xFrGuyw1V8s). 1976.
3. [Wild Cherry, "Play That Funky Music"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHcYFxU4fMo). 1976.
4. [The Trammps, "Disco Inferno"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pG8TyIEAqps). 1976.

## 2. Apple II, 1977

**Sound bites**

- [Introducing Apple II, 1977 TV spot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UL7urMIB0G0&t=4s), 0:04–0:32. "Introducing Apple II, the easy to operate home computer. Just hook it up to your TV…" and, at [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UL7urMIB0G0&t=24s), "It's called Apple II, the personal computer." A computer store's advert (High Technology), not Apple's own. Fan upload.
- [Apple II TV spot, 1979](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zd3RhRz7e2M), 0:00–0:30. "Watch what you can do with this Apple II personal computer and any colour TV…" Another store's advert (The Computer Factory). Fan upload.

**Songs**

1. [Donna Summer, "I Feel Love"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yEbaeLv-aOo). 1977, the electronic dance record of the decade.
2. [Bee Gees, "Stayin' Alive"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNFzfwLM72c). 1977.
3. [ELO, "Mr. Blue Sky"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQUlA8Hcv4s). 1977.
4. [Kraftwerk, "Trans-Europe Express"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kv8_EZrNhpY). 1977. Fan upload of the original video.

## 9. Apple Watch, 2016

Apple Watch Series 2 was announced on 7 September 2016 and went on sale on 16 September. (The first Apple Watch shipped in April 2015.)

**Sound bites**

- [Apple Event, 7 September 2016](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NS0txu_Kzl8&t=1931s), 32:11–32:25. Jeff Williams: "The first Apple Watch was splashproof, and Apple Watch Series 2 is swimproof." Apple's channel. Timed from the auto-captions, ±0.5 s.
- [Same event](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NS0txu_Kzl8&t=1922s), 32:02–32:11. "We call it Apple Watch Series 2, and it has been completely re-engineered."
- [Apple Event, 9 September 2014](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=38IqQpwPe7s&t=3338s), 55:38–55:52. Tim Cook: "But we're not quite finished yet. We have one more thing." The first Apple Watch. The word "thing" is missing from the captions, so the end is unconfirmed.

**Songs**

1. ★ [Nina Simone, "Sinnerman"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4IACZdwwJI4). "Go Time", the Apple Watch Series 2 launch advert, September 2016.
2. ★ [Beyoncé ft. Kendrick Lamar, "Freedom"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7FWF9375hUA). The "Live Bright" Series 2 advert; from Lemonade, 2016.
3. [Sia, "The Greatest"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKSRyLdjsPA). Sia performed it at the 7 September 2016 event.
4. [The Chainsmokers ft. Halsey, "Closer"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0zGcUoRlhmw). The hit of autumn 2016.
