CoDESINE

Code is a medium for art, for design. Historically, different inventions have opened up newer forms of art. Computational art has been a small field for a few decades now, but in language models we now have machines that can write code. This has reduced the barrier for people to express themselves more fluidly in this medium. In October 2026, as I write this, the abstractions at play for this medium are still being explored. I’ll share some of my experiments here.
